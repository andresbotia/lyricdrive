# LyricDrive Support

## Getting Started

1. Open LyricDrive on your iPhone.
2. Tap **Connect Spotify**.
3. Start playing a song in Spotify.
4. LyricDrive follows the currently playing track automatically.

## Using CarPlay

Open LyricDrive in CarPlay. **Now Playing** opens the system playback experience, with music controls and track information. **Lyrics** displays synchronized lyrics when available and updates automatically as songs change.

## Spotify won't reconnect

Spotify may need to be reopened after your phone or vehicle has been inactive. Return to LyricDrive and use **Reconnect Spotify** if needed.

## Lyrics aren't available

Lyrics availability varies by song. A track may have synchronized lyrics, plain lyrics, or no lyrics available from LyricDrive's providers.

## Safety

Set up LyricDrive before driving. Use CarPlay controls only when conditions allow, and always keep your attention on the road.
